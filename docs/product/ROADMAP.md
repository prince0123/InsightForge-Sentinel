Vision

Build the world's most explainable and intelligent data quality platform.

Mission

Help organizations trust their data before making business decisions.

Product Principles

I think Sentinel should always follow these principles.

Explainability

Every result should answer:

What failed?
Why?
Business impact?
Recommended action?
Automation

The user shouldn't have to manually define everything.

Sentinel should infer:

schema
business types
suggested rules
Extensibility

Everything should be pluggable.

Connectors
Rule Packs
Validators
Reporters
AI Ready

Every engine should expose structured outputs that AI systems can consume.
